# Project Methodology

## Objective

The purpose of this project was to turn a transactional sales dataset into an interactive Excel dashboard that allows business performance to be assessed quickly across revenue, costs, profit, products, sales representatives, cities and time.

## Analytical Workflow

1. **Reviewed the source dataset**
   - Checked the available fields and business dimensions.
   - Identified the measures needed for revenue, COGS, profit and profitability analysis.

2. **Structured the data in Excel**
   - Organised the transactional records into a consistent tabular format.
   - Used dedicated month fields to support time-based analysis.

3. **Built business KPIs**
   - Total Revenue
   - Total COGS
   - Total Profit
   - Profit Margin
   - Number of Customers

4. **Created PivotTable analysis**
   - Profit by Product
   - Revenue by Sales Representative
   - COGS by City
   - Profit by Month

5. **Designed the interactive dashboard**
   - Converted the PivotTable outputs into visual summaries.
   - Added slicers to make the dashboard interactive.
   - Used KPI cards to surface the most important business measures immediately.

6. **Interpreted the outputs**
   - Identified the highest-performing sales representative.
   - Identified the most profitable product.
   - Compared cost performance across cities.
   - Evaluated monthly profit performance.

## Business Questions Answered

- What is the overall revenue generated by the business?
- How much of that revenue is consumed by cost of goods sold?
- What is the resulting profit and profit margin?
- Which products generate the most profit?
- Which sales representatives generate the most revenue?
- Which cities carry the highest COGS?
- Which months perform best for profit?

## Key Results

- Total Revenue: **£2.328B**
- Total COGS: **£1.863B**
- Total Profit: **£465.674M**
- Profit Margin: **20.0%**
- Customers represented: **121**
- Highest revenue sales representative: **Peter (£434.805M)**
- Highest profit product: **Laptop A13 (£105.336M)**
- Highest COGS city: **Lagos (£493.812M)**
- Strongest profit month: **March (£126.688M)**
